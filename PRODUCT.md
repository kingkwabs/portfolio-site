# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering managers, founders, and technical collaborators evaluating Kwabena Appiah for full-stack, AI, and product-engineering opportunities. They need to understand his judgment, technical range, projects, and readiness to ship useful systems quickly.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Kwabena as a Cornell computer science engineer who builds intelligent workflow systems, AI planning tools, and algorithm-heavy applications. Success means a visitor can quickly see proof of skill, inspect the work, open the resume, and contact him with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Precise, kinetic, systems-minded. The site should feel alive and technically confident without sliding into generic neon startup visuals or crypto-coded excess.

## Anti-references

Avoid cyberpunk dashboards, purple-blue gradient hero templates, glassmorphism, identical icon-card grids, huge stats-only hero sections, resume walls of text, and decorative visuals that do not explain the work.

## Design Principles

- Make the work feel operational: projects should read like running systems, not static portfolio tiles.
- Use intelligence as a visual language: networks, state, signals, and modular panels should clarify what Kwabena builds.
- Preserve recruiter clarity: every ambitious visual move must make the resume and contact path easier to understand.
- Keep proof close to claims: pair capabilities with concrete projects, stack details, and measurable outcomes.
- Borrow Frontify's confidence: spacious white surface, crisp product modules, calm hierarchy, and a dark demo panel as the focal point.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, semantic HTML, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts that keep text readable on mobile.
